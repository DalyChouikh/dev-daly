## Concepts

- **A — Daly signature, recommended:** custom lowercase `daly.` with a matching d-and-dot favicon. It keeps the person's name prominent and sits naturally in the approved editorial page. Round forms receive a small overshoot; shared 40-unit stems and an open counter preserve the heavy character. The square symbol is intentionally positioned slightly above geometric center.
- **B — DC inset:** an open C inside a D perimeter, paired with the custom wordmark. More formal and compact. After viewing the first render, the perimeter was reduced to 28 units and the C to 24 units to open the gap between them; the internal letter is subtler at 16px.
- **C — Cut D:** one 45-degree cut shapes a solid D and its counter. The cut is echoed in the wordmark's d/l ascenders and square period. This has the simplest symbol silhouette and a more architectural character.

The wordmark's y geometry was refined to consistent 60-degree diagonals, and its period was brought closer after the first rendered review. All meaningful source iterations are retained.
